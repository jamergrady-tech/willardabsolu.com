About Us

An independent house for niche & premium fragrance.

Willard Absolu International is a newly established distributor serving trade buyers across North America. We work with distributors, wholesalers and retailers who value the right bottle over the most bottles.

Our work is quiet by design: fewer lots, chosen with care, checked before release and placed with the accounts best suited to move them. We intend to stay small enough to know every account, and exact enough that every offer means what it says.
