About us

Who we are

Rare scents, sourced quietly.

Request allocationWhatsApp the desk

Willard Absolu International is an independent trading house for niche and premium fragrance. We serve distributors, wholesalers, retailers and private trade buyers who value the right bottle over the most bottles. Our work is quiet by design: fewer lots, chosen with care, placed in the right hands.

Scarce lots rarely reach the open market. We source them through private channels, then allocate each one to the buyers best placed to move it.

Verification comes before release. Every unit is batch-code checked and video-inspected, and sealed and tester stock is clearly graded, so each offer states plainly what is in the box.

The Allocation Desk is our invite-only circle. Members receive the first look at scarce lots, before they are offered more widely.

Our mission

We bring rare niche and premium fragrance to trusted trade buyers with integrity, verification and discretion, and open private access to scarce lots that rarely reach the open market.
